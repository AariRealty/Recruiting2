# Customize a C&C Post in Canva

Turn a Coffee & Contracts post into a finished, on-brand Canva design plus a ready-to-post caption. Read the agent's `brand-brain.md` first for their voice, market, and ideal client. Use the Canva connector tools per `references/canva-flow.md`.

## Step 1 — Pick the post
Suggest a relevant post from the agent's C&C content (this week's drop, their schedule, or the library), or use a link they give you. Confirm which post before proceeding.

## Step 2 — Get it into their Canva (one click by the agent)
The C&C template lives in Coffee & Contracts' Canva account, so the connector **cannot open or copy it directly** (it returns "Not allowed to access design"). So: **send the agent the post's Canva link and ask them to click "Use template,"** which drops an editable copy into their own Canva. Ask them to paste back the link to *their* copy. Work from that copy's design ID from here on.

## Step 3 — Pick the style, drop the rest
Open the copy's pages. C&C designs usually include two style options — **light roast** and **dark roast**. Show the agent both and ask which they want. Then keep only that style: copy the design selecting just that style's page numbers into a clean new design, so the other style is dropped.

## Step 4 — Customize the text
Fully customize the design's text for the agent — fill every placeholder with real, well-known specifics for their market (neighborhoods, spots, events), in their brand-brain voice. Pull from what you already know and research the local area; only ask the agent for things that are truly theirs (a listing's price/address, a client's name). Apply edits inside an editing transaction and commit them. Never tell the agent it's saved before you commit.

## Step 5 — Photos
Ask the agent to upload their own photos, or offer to search free stock images and place them. Tip to share: suggest they keep a **Canva folder of their brand photos** so you can pull from it on their behalf next time. Insert/replace images inside the transaction, then commit.

## Step 6 — Hand over the caption
Customize the post's caption fully in their voice for their market (no placeholders, per the brand brain) and give it to them ready to post — alongside the link to their finished Canva design.

## Rules
- **Always copy before editing.** Never modify a design the agent didn't ask you to change.
- Fully customize — real local specifics, no leftover `[placeholders]` except truly private details.
- Confirm the **style choice** and the **photo source** with the agent; fill everything else yourself.
- Changes are draft until committed — always commit, and only then tell the agent it's done.
