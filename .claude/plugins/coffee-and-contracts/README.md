# Content Manager

Turn your Coffee & Contracts account into a weekly content engine. This plugin gives Claude a skill that builds your **Weekly Content Dashboard** — what's working, this week's posts customized to your voice, a content strategy, and off-social ideas — and delivers it as both a chat digest and a visual page.

## What's inside

**Weekly Content Dashboard** — pulls from your connected Coffee & Contracts account and produces four sections:

1. **What's working** — last week's Instagram performance, in plain English
2. **Content menu** — this week's content drop + your scheduled posts, every one customized to your voice and market, ready to post
3. **Content strategy** — concrete suggestions for the week based on what's performing
4. **This week's extras** — off-social ideas (emails, Stories, lead magnets) from your content vault

**Customize a Post in Canva** — takes a C&C post, has you drop it into your Canva with one "Use template" click, then picks your roast style (light or dark), customizes the text for your market, helps with photos, and hands you the finished caption.

## Setup

1. **Connect your Coffee & Contracts account.** Install the Coffee & Contracts MCP Connector from the Claude integrations directory. Once connected, Claude can reach your account directly — no manual API key needed.
2. **Add your brand brain** (recommended). The dashboard customizes everything to your voice using your `brand-brain.md`. Without it, it falls back to your Coffee & Contracts market profile. (The brand brain is built by the Real Estate Agent Essentials plugin.)

## Using it

- **On demand:** ask Claude for your *weekly content dashboard* or *"what should I post this week."*
- **Every Sunday:** ask Claude to run it as a scheduled task on Sundays around 5pm, and your dashboard will be waiting for you each week.

## Notes

- The visual dashboard page is a weekly snapshot — it's generated fresh each run, not a live view.
- Works best alongside the **Real Estate Agent Essentials** plugin (brand brain + assistant).
