Common questions

Questions agents ask before they join.

What does it cost to join?
Your monthly fee depends on your plan: $59 Mentorship (75/25), $79 Growth (85/15), or $99 Max (100%). Every plan carries a $199/year E&O and compliance fee. No franchise fees, no surprise charges.
When do I start paying?
Your first month and annual compliance fee are set up at onboarding, and your monthly runs on the same day each month after. Everything is on card so you never chase an invoice.
What splits do you offer?
75/25 Mentorship for agents who want a full support environment, 85/15 Growth for more independence, and 100% Max for fully self-managed agents. You pick the structure that fits how you work.
Is transaction coordination really included?
Yes. Mentorship agents get transaction coordination done for you. Every file across the brokerage gets broker-level compliance review before close, no matter your plan.
How fast can I get started?
Most agents are onboarded and live within a few days of applying, license transfer permitting. You apply, we set up your systems, and you're ready to write.
Why does broker-owned matter?
You work directly with the qualifying broker who built the systems and is still in the day to day. When a contract, negotiation, or a situation you haven't seen before comes up, you have access, not a group chat.
