Questions

The stuff you're actually wondering.

What are the splits, and what will this actually cost me?+
Pick your structure: 85/15 or 100 percent on a flat monthly. A $199 annual E and O and compliance fee, due when you join. Transaction fees are $499 residential and $299 vacant land. No franchise fees, and no surprise fees. Everything is spelled out before you sign.
What support do I actually get?+
A licensed Florida broker who picks up, transaction coordination through Aari Transactions, a compliance review on every file, and the full stack loaded on day one, including SkySlope, FGCMLS, and more.
How does billing work?+
Fees run through SkySlope Books. A card on file is strongly recommended. You get a 10 day grace period, then a $25 late fee. Late payments can lead to license deactivation, so keep it current.
Can I move up to a better split later?+
Yes. After five clean closings you can request a move up, subject to broker approval. Your structure should fit your business, not trap you in it.
How fast can I join?+
Minutes, not weeks. Pick a plan, review and e-sign your independent contractor agreement, put a card on file, and you land in your agent portal, same day in most cases.
