Posting Strategy

Data-driven posting recommendations for @aari.realty based on 2026 real estate Instagram benchmarks for boutique brokerages.

Core Framework
The Aari Posting Playbook
For accounts under 1K followers, consistency beats virality. Four quality posts per week with strong hooks and local relevance will outperform seven generic posts every time.
Frequency
→ Post 4–5 times per week → Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday → Best times: 9–11 AM (local) or 12–1 PM → Sunday and Monday: rest, plan, and batch-create content → Never go more than 3 days without posting
Format Mix
→ Carousels: 2–3 per week (highest saves, best for education and local content) → Reels: 1–2 per week (highest reach, best for listings and relocation) → Static posts: 1 per week max (lowest engagement but easy to batch) → Stories: daily when possible (keeps you visible in the tray)
Hook Formula
Every caption needs a strong first line — it's the only thing visible before "...more." Strong hooks for real estate: → "Here's what nobody tells you about buying in [market]" → "Stop scrolling if you're thinking about selling in 2026" → "This [city] home just hit the market and it won't last" → "$[PRICE] in [City A] vs. [City B] — look at the difference" → "3 mistakes first-time buyers make in Florida (and how to avoid them)" Weak hooks to avoid: ✗ "Happy Monday!" ✗ "Check out this listing!" ✗ "New blog post!" ✗ Starting with a hashtag
Growth Tactics
→ Engage with 10–15 local accounts daily (comment, not just like) → Follow local businesses, restaurants, and community pages in your market → Use location tags on EVERY post (Lehigh Acres, Fort Myers, Cape Coral) → Respond to every comment within 2 hours → Collaborate with local businesses for cross-promotion → Use "Save this for later" as a CTA — saves signal value to the algorithm → Repost agent wins and closings to your brokerage account
