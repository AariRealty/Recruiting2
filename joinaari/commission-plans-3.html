Questions, answered.

Is there a cap or levels to unlock?+
No. Aari has no cap, no tiers, and nothing to “unlock.” Your split is your split from the day you join. What you see is what you get.
Is this revenue share or a downline?+
No. Aari is not a revenue-share or recruiting-downline model. You are not paid on other people’s deals, and you do not need to recruit anyone. You earn on your own production.
So what exactly do I pay?+
Three flat things: your monthly fee ($79 Growth or $99 Max), a $199 annual E&O and compliance fee, and a flat per-side transaction fee ($499 residential, $299 vacant land). That is the whole picture.
What is included?+
The SkySlope Suite, a CRM, and real broker support — on both plans. Growth also gives you Aari Transactions at half price on full-file coordination: $199.50 a side instead of $399. On Max you pay the standard rate.
Can I change plans later?+
Yes. After five closed residential transactions with fully compliant files, you can request a plan change in writing, subject to broker approval. Approved changes take effect the first of the following month.
