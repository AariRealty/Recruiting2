Strategic Recommendations

3 Things Working — Keep Doing These

  1. Reels with a real person on camera outperform static graphics 2:1 in engagement. Every top-5 post is a Reel with someone talking.
  2. Posting discipline is strong. 6+ posts/week, zero gaps over 3 days. Protect this habit.
  3. The Babcock Ranch / Tucker's Cove local series builds a recognizable narrative. Worth extending to other growth corridors.

3 Biggest Opportunities

  1. Bio is 100% recruiting, 0% consumer. Split the message: bio speaks to the consumer, a highlight speaks to agents.
  2. Following > followers. Run an unfollow audit — five-minute credibility fix with zero downside.
  3. Relocation (0%) and Social Proof (4%) are both core pillars and both effectively unpublished. Lowest-cost content types to produce.
